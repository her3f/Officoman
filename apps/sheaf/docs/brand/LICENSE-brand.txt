Officoman brand assets
======================

The files in this directory — the Officoman mark and wordmark — are the project's
own artwork, drawn for Officoman in 2026. They are not the upstream ArtCraft or
Storyteller marks; those were removed during the rebrand because their terms require
forks and modified versions to delete them.

These marks are distributed under the same licence as the rest of Officoman:
MIT or Apache-2.0, at your option. If you fork Officoman, you may keep them or
replace them with your own.
