OUR STORY

A new desk.
Open foundations.

White Office is an independent fork of four Crafting Apps. A new identity, familiar file formats, and source code you can explore.

Built on the work of others

Nib, Tally, Podium and Sheaf are derived from the projects below, published by the ArtCraft team and their contributors. The source is dual-licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0; third-party components keep their respective licences.
