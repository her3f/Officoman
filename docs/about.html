What changed

Names

Every product name replaced, including the crate names, binaries, window titles and CLI namespaces.

Identifiers

Bundle identifiers moved from ai.storyteller.* to io.github.her3f.* across desktop files, Flatpak, AppStream, WiX and macOS packaging.

Icons

Every app icon redrawn from scratch. The upstream icons and brand assets were removed, as their terms require.

Links

Site, store, Discord, YouTube and social links now point at this project.

Trademarks

ArtCraft, DeckCraft, GridCraft, PdfCraft, WordCraft and their logos are trademarks of the ArtCraft Team. They are not covered by the source licences, and White Office does not use them. White Office is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the ArtCraft Team. Naming upstream in this page is how the licence asks to be credited — nothing more.

Still third-party

The build depends on libraries that live under the same upstream organisation (craft-libs, egui, craft-fonts, craft-i18n). Those are third-party dependencies, pinned by revision, and their names are left untouched on purpose: renaming them would break resolution. Their licences are recorded in each app's ATTRIBUTION.md.
